# Home landing page

Mode: Persuade. One Uzbek school introduction with eight sections. Readers should understand the school's atmosphere, explore school life, and find contact information. All photos, staff names, numbers, and achievements are demo material.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A welcoming school prospectus, using substantial photography and open editorial composition rather than a sequence of identical feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory page ground, deep forest-green ink and a substantial green learning section, muted straw accents. Expressive Bodoni Moda display lettering with Manrope body copy. Rectangular photography and restrained rounded controls.

STORY: Introduce the school, describe learning, meet the demo staff, explore activities and highlights, view photos, and reach contact details. Anchor navigation keeps the whole experience on one page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Per the user’s explicit refinement, an edge-to-edge classroom photograph covers the full hero, including behind the transparent masthead. Light Uzbek display copy and a paper-colored discovery action overlay a static dark scrim. Desktop keeps an asymmetric headline/supporting-copy arrangement; phones stack the copy over the same full background. The masthead becomes ivory after the hero or when its mobile menu opens. The photograph opens with a subtle GSAP scale settle, while the headline and action arrive in one coordinated sequence. Later motion responds to visitors through control feedback and learning-panel changes; no scroll-jacking or repeated section entrances.

FORM: User-approved ivory/forest-green editorial school prospectus. The user pinned the direction and chose code-first. No catalog roll was run under new-work.md's pinned-direction exception; mode-persuade.md supplied the applicable mode rules. No seed reference or generated comp exists.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Open decisions: all real school data, final logo, school contacts, location, and final photographs.

Contact refinement: the user requested specific example contact details. The forest contact section includes street/house/district/postal code, a telephone link, and Telegram/Instagram profile links, with a visible demo disclosure. Real contact details remain an open decision.
